Programs

A · Direct mail

Renew DPVA donors, then test prospect mail by score

  • Renewal and reactivation: mail all 27,819 top-10% DPVA donors. The 18,469 who last gave before 2022 are the biggest win-back pool on the file.
  • Prospect test: mail the 146,951 Donor Score 96–100 households with no matched gift. Hold back a random 10% as an unmailed control so the lift is measurable.
  • Roll out by band: add 91–95 (225,452 households) only if the 96–100 cell beats breakeven. Leave voters scored 1–70 off prospect mail. They are 70% of voters but almost none give (0.7%).
  • Package: one household piece addressed to the highest-scoring voter at the address, with a reply device carrying the ask string.
B · Digital and text

Phones and ad audiences, since cold prospects have almost no emails

  • The gap: only 25 of the 166,316 top-5% prospects have an email that is not already on DPVA's email list. Email fundraising works for DPVA's own ActBlue donors, but it cannot reach new prospects without an email append.
  • Subscribers who never gave: 25,615 top-5% prospects (51,726 in the top 10%) are on DPVA's email list but have no matched gift. They already get DPVA's email asks, so they are not counted as email prospects. The email program can sort them by score for a first-gift ask.
  • Peer-to-peer text: 68,960 top-5% prospects have a number VAN flags as a cell (most VAN "cell phones" are flagged likely landlines). Send a short ask with an ActBlue link, tested in waves of 10–20K by score band.
  • Paid social matched audiences: upload name, address and cell for the top 5% to Meta and Google. Seed lookalikes from DPVA's own donors only.
  • Monthly upgrade: 434 of the 4,377 active top-10% DPVA donors already give monthly. Ask the rest to switch to $10, $15 or $25 a month.
  • Email append: a vendor can add emails for the 140,618 top-5% prospects with no usable email. Price it against the text and ads results first.
C · Call time, events, phones

Other ways the score helps

  • Call time: 3,007 top-10% DPVA donors have given $250 or more in one gift. Rank candidate and chair call sheets by score, then by highest gift.
  • House parties and events: pick hosts and invite lists by locality. Fairfax, Arlington, Loudoun, Chesterfield and Henrico hold the most top-10% prospects.
  • Telefundraising: 94% of top-5% prospects have a phone in VAN, so a paid phone program can reach the households mail does not convert.
  • Suppression: drop voters scored 1–70 from every cold prospect list to save postage and ad spend.
  • 2027 General Assembly: the State/Local Donor Score finds likely state givers for HD and Senate candidates' own prospecting.

How the numbers work
  • Cutting the prospect list drops the lowest-scored households first, so the people left are likelier to give. The rise follows the model's donor probabilities for these prospects. It is a guide until a test measures the real curve.
  • A donor household counts in the mail row if anyone there has given to a DPVA direct-mail appeal. Otherwise it counts in the group of its most recent DPVA gift. Counts update with the mix.
  • New donors are the prospect cell's gifts. Prospect mail that loses money can still pay off, because new donors renew later. Judge it on cost per new donor as well as first-mailing net.

How the numbers work
  • Cost is every message sent: numbers × messages per number × cost per message. Undelivered messages still cost.
  • Prospect gifts = numbers × delivered × click-through × give rate of clicks. Cutting the list keeps the highest scores, so click-through rises along the model's donor probabilities for these numbers (the same curve as the mail calculator).
  • Each message loses the opt-out share of delivered numbers. Those numbers cannot be texted again, which is the cost of a heavy send that the net does not show.
  • Only about a third of VAN's CellPhone values are flagged as cells, so the textable list is much smaller than "has a cell phone" suggests.

Run it as a test first

  1. Pull lists from VOTER_DONOR_SCORES: DPVA donors by VANID, prospects with no VA_DONORS row, Donor Score 96–100, one piece per household. The calculator's Export button writes this query.
  2. Split each list at random: 90% mailed or texted, 10% held back as a control. Code each cell by score band and ask string.
  3. After 90 days, compare response and average gift by band against the control. That gives the true lift of the score. DPVA's past mail drops had 75% of their gifts in by 30 days, 87% by 60 and 91% by 90. A 60-day read should be divided by 0.87.
  4. Load responders back into NGP so the next model build learns from them, then roll out to the bands that paid.
