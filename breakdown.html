How the scores are built

  • One gradient-boosted model per score, trained on VAN voter data: Dem support, GOTV and PartyPoints scores, age, sex, race, registration and address tenure, vote history, Dem and Rep primary counts, phone and email on file, household size, county, media market, districts, the precinct's donor rate, and DNC education and volunteer scores.
  • A donor is a matched voter with a Dem-side gift since 2022-01-01: DPVA (ActBlue and NGP), any ActBlue gift, a Democratic candidate or party committee, or a PAC whose donors give almost only to Democrats.
  • Scores are statewide percentiles (1–100). About a third of real donors never matched to a voter, so they count as non-donors when the model trains.
  • Source: Finance.dbo.VOTER_DONOR_SCORES, built 2026-10-20 by build_donor_scores.py. These numbers are a snapshot and do not update on their own.
